# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct / 贡献者行为准则

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality,
personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

我们承诺让每一位参与者都能在不受骚扰的环境中参与社区，不因年龄、体型、可见或
不可见的残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育、社会经济地位、
国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向而受到区别对待。

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards / 行为标准

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the overall
  community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of
  any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others’ private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Enforcement responsibilities / 执行责任

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of
acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in
response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or
harmful.

Community leaders have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, wiki edits, issues, and other contributions that are not
aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation
decisions when appropriate.

## Scope / 适用范围

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, including GitHub issues,
pull requests, discussions, and project chat, and also applies when an individual
is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement / 举报与处理

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers. Please use a private channel: contact
[@Peteliao](https://github.com/Peteliao) through GitHub, or open a minimal public
issue asking for a private follow-up **without** including sensitive details.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers
are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.

Do not report security vulnerabilities here. Use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

如遇骚扰或不当行为，请通过 GitHub 私下联系 [@Peteliao](https://github.com/Peteliao)，
或先提交一个不含敏感细节的公开 Issue 请求私下沟通。维护者会尽快、公正地处理，
并尊重举报者的隐私。安全漏洞请按 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) 私密报告。

## Enforcement guidelines / 处理准则

1. **Correction / 纠正** — A private warning explaining the violation, with context
   for why the behavior was inappropriate.
2. **Warning / 警告** — A warning with consequences for continued behavior,
   including a period of no interaction with the people involved.
3. **Temporary ban / 临时封禁** — A temporary ban from any interaction with the
   community.
4. **Permanent ban / 永久封禁** — A permanent ban from community participation.
